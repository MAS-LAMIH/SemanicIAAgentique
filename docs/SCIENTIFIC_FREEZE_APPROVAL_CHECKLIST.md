## Change control after approval

Freeze generic resolver/validation/shared mappings/transport/Goal/native truth
rules/cost collector/tests and the allowed integration interface together. Permit
only approved provider-local additions expressing supported semantics. No
post-unsealing generic primitive, prompt/interface, oracle, task-answer or repair-
rule change can retain the original confirmatory-baseline claim. A discovered
core defect stops affected runs; preserve failures and costs, independently
classify it, issue a new candidate and disclose contamination. Same-provider
reruns after exposure are exploratory unless an appropriate genuinely new holdout
and predeclared restart design are available.
